# Notation correspondence

The functions are real-valued on real time. `Tendsto ... atTop (𝓝 0)` means
convergence as time tends to infinity. The formal derivative is an explicit
`HasDerivAt` witness; the integral is Mathlib’s interval integral.
Dimension: scalar time and scalar trajectory value. Type: real-valued function and derivative. Frame: no spatial frame is present. Sign: derivative or integral hypotheses use the exact nonpositive and finite-limit signs. Unit: abstract scalar units. Lean: Barbalat and KhalilComparison encode the endpoints.
