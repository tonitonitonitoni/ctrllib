# Notation correspondence

Euclidean continuous linear maps are rendered as matrices acting on column
vectors. The Lean norm is rendered as $\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}$ and transpose as
$A^\mathsf{T}$.

Eigenvalue floors and singular-value ceilings are scalar spectral certificates.
Dimension: finite-dimensional Euclidean domain and codomain. Type: continuous linear maps and real operator norm. Frame: Euclidean orthonormal frame is mathematical, while physical frame is external. Sign: norm bounds and eigenvalue floors are nonnegative, with positive floor where inversion is claimed. Unit: operator units are abstract. Lean: EuclideanNormBridge declarations carry the types.
