## Evidence and dependencies


## Inputs

- A finite nonempty index type n and the Euclidean state z = (v,e)
- Constant real matrices M, D, and K defining M vdot + D v + K e = 0 and edot = v
- The operator-exponential flow Phi(t,z) generated by the block field
- The mechanical energy V(v,e) = one-half v^T M v + one-half e^T K e

## Assumptions

- M, D, and K are symmetric positive definite on the finite-dimensional task space
- The equation is unforced, autonomous, and constant; no variable gains, contacts, saturation, or sampling are included
- The physical plant, controller, frames, units, and runtime implementation are not identified by the generic matrices

## Outputs

### Linear operator exponential flow

For a bounded linear operator A on a real Banach space, the flow Psi_A(t,z) = exp(t A) z has the stated derivative and solves the field.

Exact declarations: `Ctrllib.linearOperatorFlow_hasDerivAt`, `Ctrllib.linearOperatorFlow_isSolutionTo`.
Proof section: [Generic exponential-flow lemma](proof.md#generic-exponential-flow-lemma).

### Matrix impedance solution

The specialized exponential flow solves the explicit field $\dot v=-M^{-1}(Dv+Ke)$, $\dot e=v$. Under positive definiteness of $M$, this is equivalent to $M\dot v+Dv+Ke=0$.

Exact declarations: `Ctrllib.matrixImpedance_isSolutionTo`.
Proof section: [Model and assumptions](proof.md#model-and-assumptions).

### Matrix impedance energy decrease

The quadratic energy is positive away from the origin and is nonincreasing along the unforced flow. The conventional calculation gives $\dot V=-v^\top Dv$; the named formal endpoint exports the resulting monotonicity property.

Exact declarations: `Ctrllib.matrixImpedanceEnergy_antitone`, `Ctrllib.matrixImpedanceEnergy_pos`.
Proof section: [Energy, decrease, and positivity](proof.md#energy-decrease-and-positivity).

### Matrix impedance lyapunov stability

The origin of the specified matrix impedance flow is Lyapunov stable.

Exact declarations: `Ctrllib.matrixImpedance_lyapStable`.
Proof section: [Energy, decrease, and positivity](proof.md#energy-decrease-and-positivity).

### Matrix impedance global convergence

Every initial state of the specified matrix impedance flow converges to the origin as t tends to positive infinity.

Exact declarations: `Ctrllib.matrixImpedance_tendsto_zero`.
Proof section: [Global convergence](proof.md#global-convergence).

### Matrix impedance global stability

The flow is Lyapunov stable and every initial state converges to the origin, as the conjunction encoded by the global endpoint.
