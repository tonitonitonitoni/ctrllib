# Discrepancies and remaining obligations

The energy derivative is semidefinite: at zero velocity and nonzero error it vanishes. Convergence therefore uses invariance, not a strict-derivative assertion.

The theorem is global on its Euclidean state space. A physical pose chart may be local. Reference cancellation, residual/contact inputs, variable gains, flexibility and allocation realization remain separate application proofs. No scalar-only or frozen-gain result is silently promoted to one of those claims.
