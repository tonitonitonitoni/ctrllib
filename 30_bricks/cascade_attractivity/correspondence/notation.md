# Notation correspondence

The Lean component variables are rendered as nonnegative norms
$r_1(t)$ and $r_2(t)$. Class-K-infinity functions are written
$\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ and act on nonnegative scalars.

Cascade denotes the encoded pair of coupled comparison trajectories.
Dimension: product state E₁ × E₂ with finite-dimensional component types as supplied. Type: two normed trajectories and real comparison values. Frame: no physical frame is assumed. Sign: component norms and class-K-infinity values are nonnegative; the decay rate is positive. Unit: abstract component and Lyapunov units. Lean: Cascade declarations encode the product type.
