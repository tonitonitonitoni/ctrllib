# Notation and Lean correspondence

Types: arbitrary X and U; dimension is not fixed or needed. Lean represents
positive horizon N as n+1, controls by Fin(n+1), states by Fin(n+2), and time
by natural numbers. The frame and physical units are application choices.
Sign: new cost <= old cost - first stage cost; no cost nonnegativity is
assumed for this lemma. Terminal feedback takes a subtype member of Xf(k).
