# Notation correspondence

The Lean Euclidean continuous linear map is rendered as a matrix $P$. Its
pairing is $\langle Px,x\rangle$ and its inverse as $P^{-1}$ when supplied.

Attractivity is distance convergence of the supplied center-of-mass trajectory.
Dimension: finite-dimensional Euclidean center-of-mass state. Type: matrix or continuous linear map, inner product, and Flow trajectory. Frame: the Euclidean frame is mathematical and physical frame semantics are external. Sign: positive-definite pairing is nonnegative and its eigenvalue floor is positive. Unit: matrix and distance units are abstract. Lean: ComLaSalleMatrix carries the exact interface.
