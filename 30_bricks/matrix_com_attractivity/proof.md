# Conventional derivation

## Matrix premises

Let $P$ be symmetric positive definite. Then

$$
\langle Px,y\rangle=\langle x,Py\rangle,\qquad
\langle Px,x\rangle\ge0,
$$

and equality in the positive quadratic form forces $x=0$. Hence the induced
continuous linear map is self-adjoint and injective; in finite dimensions it
has an inverse. The dot-product definition endpoint records the equality
between the abstract pairing and matrix multiplication.

## Attractivity conclusion

Insert those matrix facts into the abstract center-of-mass LaSalle argument.
The Lyapunov pairing is nonnegative and has the required zero set; the supplied
flow solution and forward-precompactness witness provide the remaining
hypotheses. The abstract omega-limit argument then shows that the
center-of-mass distance tends to the target set. This conclusion inherits
every trajectory and derivative premise of the abstract theorem.
