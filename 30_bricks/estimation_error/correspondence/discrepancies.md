# Discrepancies and open items

No discrepancy was found in the reviewed local derivation. The minus sign on measurement correction, common known control and nonnegative factors used in the norm bound are explicit. The contraction condition is an operator-norm bound, not an eigenvalue claim.

Open application items are the physical state/measurement maps, sensor and actuator timing, norm/unit scaling, initial error, residual envelopes, linearization domain, observer choice and the loss's Lipschitz domain. A probability bound must not be substituted for an almost-sure premise. Physical validation and scientific acceptance remain separate.
